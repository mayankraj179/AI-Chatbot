# Northwind Fictional Ltd — Leave Policy

This policy applies to all permanent employees of Northwind Fictional Ltd. It is fictional sample content.

## Annual leave

Every employee earns 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per completed month.
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Casual leave

Employees get 8 days of casual leave per year for short personal matters. Casual leave cannot be
carried over and cannot be combined with annual leave for more than 5 consecutive working days.

## Sick leave

Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences
longer than 2 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Partners receive 4 weeks of paid leave, to be
taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## How to apply

Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for annual leave.
Casual and sick leave may be recorded on the day itself.
