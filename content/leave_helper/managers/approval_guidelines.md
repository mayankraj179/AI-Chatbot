# Leave Approval Guidelines for Managers

Fictional sample content, visible only to managers (realm role chatbot-admin in the dev realm).

## Approval deadlines

Managers must approve or reject annual leave requests within 3 working days. Requests not actioned
within 3 working days are escalated to the department head automatically.

## Team coverage rule

No more than 2 members of the same team may be on annual leave at the same time during the
quarter-end freeze (the last 10 working days of each quarter).
