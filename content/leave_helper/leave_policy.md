# Leave Policy

Northwind Fictional Ltd. This policy applies to all permanent employees. Fictional sample content.

## Earned leave

Every employee earns 10 days of earned leave per year. Unused earned leave is carried forward to the next
year, up to a carry-forward cap of 25 days. Earned leave above the cap is forfeited at year end.

## Casual leave

Employees get 8 casual leave days per year for short personal matters. Casual leave cannot be carried
forward: unused casual leave days lapse at the end of the year.

## Applying for leave

Apply for earned leave in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance. Casual leave may be recorded on
the day itself.
